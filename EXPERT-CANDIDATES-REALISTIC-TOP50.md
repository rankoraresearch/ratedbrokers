## Methodology

Composite `realismScore` (0–100):

| Factor | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Credentials | 25% | CFA / CFP / FINRA / CMT / CPA — verified ✓ beats unverified claim beats no cert |
| On-topic | 25% | forex / cfd / broker-safety / regulation beats stocks beats crypto beats generic finance |
| Reach (capped) | 15% | Sweet spot 10–200K combined followers — penalty above 500K (celebrity = unreachable) |
| Byline depth | 15% | yearsInIndustry + number of publishing outlets |
| Approachability | 20% | Contributor/freelance > senior/editor > chief at Tier-1 |

**Penalties** (subtracted from total):
- Chief/staff at Bloomberg, Reuters, WSJ, FT, CNBC, NYT — −20 (unlikely to moonlight)
- Twitter followers > 500K — −15 (out of our league)
- No LinkedIn AND no email — −15 (no path to reach them)
- Harvest badge = C — −10

**Classification buckets** (within top 50):

- **A — Perfect fit**: verified core cert ✓ + on-topic beat + approachable
- **B — Strong realistic**: verified core cert ✓ + approachable (topic adjacent)
- **C — Upside bet**: on-topic + approachable + some cert claim (needs verification)
- **D — Secondary**: approachable + good byline, no cert
- **E — Stretch**: high-authority but harder sell

## Notes on methodology tradeoffs

1. **Celebrity penalty is deliberate.** Scott Melker (1M X), Lyn Alden (908K X), Whitney Webb (689K X) all drop out of top 50 by design — they get paid tens of thousands for a keynote, not for a broker review byline. If RatedBrokers pivots to a budget that can reach them, this cap should be lifted.

2. **Unverified cert claims** stay counted at 70% weight. The CFA Institute directory closed public lookup, so many legitimate CFA/CFP holders appear as `verified: false` in our data. Dropping them to 0 would over-penalise real credential holders.

3. **On-topic beat** beats generic personal-finance. A CFP who writes about retirement planning scores lower than a CMT who writes about forex — even though the CFP may be more prestigious in absolute terms. Right trade-off for *broker review* content specifically.

4. **Contact gate.** 42/50 top picks have LinkedIn. 9/50 have public email. Outreach Wave 1 should lead with LinkedIn InMail + Muck Rack profile mentions; cold email is secondary.

5. **What's not measured yet**: actual price (compensation expectations), conflict-of-interest (already advising a broker?), current availability. All three need manual triage per candidate before outreach.
