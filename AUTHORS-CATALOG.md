## Score formula

`finalScore = baseScore × (1 + authoritativeness/100)`

- **Base** (0-100): outletDR × 0.35 + badge + seniority + LinkedIn + MuckRack + Twitter + email + certs × 4 + multi-outlet + competitor-linked outlet
- **Authoritativeness** (0-50): certs × 8 + years (cap 10) + tier-1 press × 3 + TV (cap 6) + books +5 + own domain +3 + multi-outlet +3 each + FINRA clean +5 (former: -5)
- **E-E-A-T tier**: S ≥ 40 auth · A ≥ 25 · B ≥ 12 · C < 12
